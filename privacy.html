Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Overview

Sub500 Archive is a personal-use utility operated by its owner. It uses rclone and Google's OAuth service to access the owner's Google Drive account for archival transfers and verification.

Google Drive data

The utility may read, create, update, and organize files in the authorized Google Drive account as required for the owner's archival workflow. Data is transferred directly between the owner's device and Google Drive through rclone.

Credentials and tokens

OAuth credentials and tokens are handled by Google and rclone. They are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration and are not collected by this website or published in this repository.

Collection, sharing, and sale

This informational website does not collect personal information, use analytics, set application cookies, sell data, or share Google Drive data with third parties. The repository does not contain archive payloads or authentication secrets.

Retention and control

Archive retention is controlled by the owner. Google account access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party connections settings, and local rclone credentials can be removed from the owner's device.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the repository issue tracker. Do not include credentials, tokens, or private archive data in an issue.
