Sub500 Archive

Sub500 Archive is a personal archival utility that uses rclone to transfer files between the owner's device and the owner's Google Drive account.

Purpose

  • Upload archive objects and manifests to Google Drive.
  • Verify uploaded file identity and integrity.
  • Support controlled local retention after successful verification.

This website is informational only. It does not accept uploads, collect credentials, or provide access to stored archive data.
